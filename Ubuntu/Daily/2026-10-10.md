#### Things to be aware of

- **More Slack alerts.** Every camera self-heal logs an ERROR, and the top-left camera was already flaky in my tests (its first stream start got no frames), so expect some.
- **Upload size.** Full frames are about 1–3 MB per camera per Scan at full resolution, on a metered link.
- **Storage names changed.** Uploaded images are now `images/img_<scan>_<role>.jpg`, the full frame, where they used to be `…_model.jpg` crops. Anything that reads them will now get full frames; records from before this change have no `cameras` field.
- **A CSI heal takes both bottom cameras down for a few seconds,** because it restarts `nvargus-daemon`. A press during any heal waits for it to finish before the Flash goes on.
- **The restart combo now also resets the top USB cameras.** The countdown appears after 5 s of the 10 s hold.
- **Room rotation is `room_rotation_deg = 90`** in `[display]`. `empty.svg` and `fire_dangerous.svg` are still in `room/`, untouched.
- **Docs.** ADR-0016 is new (full-frame upload), and ADR-0013 has an amendment saying the bottom cameras are CSI. `CONTEXT.md`, the README and `AGENTS.md` are updated to match.
