1. The CSI cameras lock up, and nothing short of a reboot fixes them. This happened twice:

- 10 Oct, 03:00 to 19:30. The outage started at the nightly restart and only cleared when the Jetson was rebooted 16 hours later.
- 11 Oct, 08:08 until at least 13:10, the most recent failure in the logs. Argus (NVIDIA's camera service) logged a hardware timeout on both sensors at once. dw-wade then crashed with a segfault (SEGV), leaving the cameras held by the dead process ("Device 0 is in use"). After that, every self-heal restart of the camera service came back with No module data found / V4L2Device not available.

So the self-heal runs every 20 minutes and accomplishes nothing. Both sensors failing together points at something they share (the camera service, the capture hardware, memory or power) rather than one bad ribbon cable.

2. The unit is short on memory. It's an 8 GB Orin NX with 1.8 GB available and 2 GB in swap. Out-of-memory kills happened at 11:44 and 13:07, and the 13:07 one killed dw-wade itself. On a Jetson the cameras' image pipeline uses the same RAM. Alongside the kiosk, the unit is running a desktop Firefox, several Claude sessions, pytest and the old wade_system scripts. Just before the 08:08 lockup, one image took 26 s to process instead of about 1 s. I can't prove memory caused the lockup, but it's the strongest suspect.

The USB extension cable

It can make the top cameras unstable. Both Arducams run at USB 2.0 speed (480 Mbit/s), which allows about 5 m of cable in total, and each camera draws up to 500 mA. A passive extension adds signal loss and voltage drop. The typical result is a stream that never starts, or a camera that drops off or comes back without its video device. That matches top_left on 10 Oct 19:18–19:40.

Since the reboot, though, the kernel shows no USB disconnects and no over-current events, and top_right hasn't failed once. To test the cable:

- Swap the two top cameras' cables and see whether the fault follows the cable or the camera.
- If it's the cable, use an active (repeater) extension, or a powered hub at the camera end.

Note that top_left isn't in the current config at all; only 3 cameras are bound.

What I'd change, most important first

1. Reboot when CSI stays down. If the cameras still fail after a camera-service restart, reboot the Jetson, at most once an hour. It needs a sudoers rule. This alone would have cut both outages from hours to minutes.
2. Free memory on the unit. Move dev work (Claude, pytest, the desktop Firefox, the old wade_system scripts) off the production Jetson. Also tell the kernel to kill dw-wade and the camera service last (OOMScoreAdjust= in the service files).
3. Clean up the nightly restart. Stop the CSI pipelines properly on shutdown and restart the camera service as part of the nightly restart. Also fix the ~45 s restart loop afterwards: port 5010 is still held when the new process starts.
4. Fix a bug in the USB reset helper. On 10 Oct top_left came up with no video device, and dw-wade-usb-reset refused to reset it with "not a camera". That's exactly the state it exists to fix. It should also accept the Arducam's USB ID (0c40:0561).
5. Keep kernel logs across reboots. There's no /var/log/journal, so all kernel USB and CSI history from before the 10 Oct reboot is gone. Creating it makes the logs persistent.
6. Fewer alerts. no frame from csi sensor N is logged at ERROR 280 times, and every ERROR line goes to Slack. One alert per Scan is enough.
