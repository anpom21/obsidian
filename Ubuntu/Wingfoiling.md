## Tips and tricks
- Toe and heal side trap
Lean a little back to avoid toe side trap but not too much to avoid the heel side trap.
- Take off mistakes
Get some speed from the air before trying to takeoff. Beginners tend to have a lot of weight on the back side increasing drag.
https://youtu.be/sz57ucIpUGA?is=KBpSrG3Qa6h6be5h
- Pumping to lift
Aim crosswind, get to maximum speed, wait for a gust. Then pump the wing or also pump with the legs to get foiling. 
https://youtu.be/ef7jnVVl9b4?is=jZzYvM1CH1kbEpth
