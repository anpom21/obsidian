**Trajectory Planning & Robotic Grasping** **–** **High-Level Key Topics (Exam Use)**

- Difference between **joint space** and **tool space** trajectories
- Common trajectory construction methods (demonstration, interpolation, splines, dynamic systems)
- **Linear interpolation** in joint and task space – advantages, limitations, and velocity/acceleration issues
- **Parabolic blends** in joint and task space – smoothing motion profiles
- **Quaternion representation** for orientation – advantages over Euler angles/rotation matrices
- **Spherical linear interpolation (SLERP)** for smooth orientation transitions
- **Jerk** in motion control and its impact on trajectory smoothness
- **Cubic polynomial interpolation** – single and multi-point, with via points
- Using **learning-based methods** to define trajectories (simulation training, block-based programming, force mode)
- Basics of **robotic grasping** – design, control, sensing, and object knowledge
- Human vs. robotic grasping – key differences and challenges
- Industrial grasping methods (form closure, force closure, combined) and grip types (external, internal, intermediate, vacuum)
- **Gripper types** – servo-electric, pneumatic, vacuum, jamming, etc.
- Contact modeling – point contact without friction, hard finger, soft finger, friction cone
- **Grasp planning** – database-driven, learning-based, shape matching approaches (GraspNet, DexNet)
- Gripper design process – parameterized and imprint-based design
- Performance metrics – success, robustness, alignment, coverage, wrench indices
- Optimization of gripper design using simulation and iterative refinement
