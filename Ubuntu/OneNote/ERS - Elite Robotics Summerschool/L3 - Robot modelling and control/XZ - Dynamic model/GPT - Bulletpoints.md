**Dynamics of Robot Manipulators** **–** **High-Level Key Topics (Exam Use)**

- Purpose of dynamics in robotics – simulation, control design, structural evaluation
- Recap of kinematics and transformation matrices
- Link description parameters: link length, link twist, link offset, joint angle
- Denavit–Hartenberg (DH) notation for kinematic modeling
- Frame attachment conventions for links (first, intermediate, last links)
- Non-uniqueness in frame assignment and link parameter choices
- Link transformation formulation and concatenation to get base-to-end transformation
- Newton–Euler formulation:
	- Outward iterations (velocities, accelerations)
	- Inward iterations (forces, torques)
	- Free-body diagram approach for revolute and prismatic joints
- Inertia tensor definition, mass distribution, and parallel-axis theorem
- Velocity propagation between links (linear & angular velocities)
- Dynamic equation structure: mass matrix, Coriolis/centrifugal, gravity terms
- Lagrangian formulation: kinetic and potential energy computation
- Comparison of Newton–Euler and Lagrange methods
- Example applications of dynamic modeling in robotic manipulators
